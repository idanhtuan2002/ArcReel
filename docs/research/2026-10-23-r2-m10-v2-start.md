## Scope and unresolved gates

ADR-0080 defines M10 as versioned quality profiles, typed multi-assessor runs,
human quality disposition, immutable packaging revisions and deterministic
export bundles. Export does not itself authorize publishing.

The user's instruction switches active work to M10 while M9 stays paused.
The older ADR and architecture contract still require accepted predecessors
before implementation. This research does not fabricate that evidence or
silently rewrite the normative documents. The design must explicitly resolve
how an export-only V2 packet can proceed independently of unfinished M9 before
its implementation manifest is released.
