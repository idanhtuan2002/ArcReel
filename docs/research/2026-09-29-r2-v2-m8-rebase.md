## Facts that bear on the re-base

- **Superseded sequencing.** ADR-0089 re-sequenced delivery (bounded V1 first) after the M8
  design. V2 authorities enter "through their accepted dependency packets, one at a time"
  (strategy §7).
- **Independence cannot come from the author.** The M8 contract requires independent
  acceptance. An acceptance written by the agent that authored the commits would not be
  independent.
- **Host changes since the design.** Since M8 was designed, the host gained:
  - the gate tables, script lock and O2a export (ADR-0090, ADR-0092);
  - the release-mode paid-route gate, budgets and the Agent cap (ADR-0091, ADR-0093);
  - the admission filters (ADR-0094).

  The M8 plan's schema and M7 symbols must be re-checked against this host.

## Options

| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **B1: an independent acceptance review of the V1→V2 lineage, then an M8 contract amendment.** | A reviewer who did not write the code reviews `r2-v1.0.0..r2-v2/main` (V1-07, V1-08, V1.x, V2-01) and writes the acceptance record with the contract's required fields. The M8 contract is then amended to name the V1/V2 evidence, the current Alembic head and PostgreSQL 16. Meets the contract's intent. Needs a reviewer: Codex, a background review agent, or a human. |
| B2: a sponsor waiver in place of independent acceptance | Faster. It moves M8 onto an unreviewed base, which is the risk the contract exists to prevent. |
| B3: re-derive M8 from scratch for V2 | Discards a completed, reviewed design, where most of its contracts are still valid. |

## Recommendation

**B1.** Until the independent review exists, the independent work is:

- the V2-01 operator surface (API and UI for pause and ceiling);
- the M8 contract amendment drafted as a proposal.

The review itself needs a reviewer other than the author, and M8 execution stays blocked on it.
