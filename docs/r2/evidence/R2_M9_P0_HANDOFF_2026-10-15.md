## P0 tooling

`scripts/r2/verify_m9_prerequisites.py` consumes that sponsor decision and the
existing M8 acceptance. It reuses `GitEvidenceReader` and duplicate-key-rejecting
JSON from the existing M9 verifier, and the Git-pinned Alembic metadata helper
already used by M7/M8. The original two-review design verifier is unchanged;
it remains the verifier for that ceremony and is not the sponsor-mode entry.

The P0 schema and verifier distinguish:

- `M9_PREREQUISITES_CANDIDATE`: structural/source checks only; no M9-A release.
- `M9_PREREQUISITES_ACCEPTED`: additionally requires a PASS, author-independent
  review bound to the exact combined base, with no findings/skips/unknowns.

The prerequisite record commit must be the sole child of its combined base and
change only `R2_M9_PREREQUISITE_ACCEPTANCE.json`. The base must descend from both
accepted inputs, preserve their hash-bound evidence, and retain the singleton
M8 migration head/graph. Schema and verifier hashes are bound at both base and
record commit. Dirty work or a checkout change during verification fails closed.
`--acceptance-ref <full SHA>` allows a later descendant worktree to verify the
immutable ceremony rather than pretending its new HEAD is the acceptance commit.
