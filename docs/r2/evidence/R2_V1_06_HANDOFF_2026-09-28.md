## Release state after V1-06

In `PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`:

- **Image, text and audio routes** (built-in, and custom per provider) are refused until the
  operator runs a canary. The canary is bound to the model the backend actually calls and to
  the provider's configuration (ADR-0091 amendment item 1), and expires after 30 days by default.
- **Async video routes** are all disabled, because every shipped adapter profile is
  `UNQUALIFIED`.
- **The Agent and trial runs** are refused.
- **Every ledgered paid call** needs a budget scope for its currency.

## Open items

- **The Agent's per-session budget reservation** is needed to allow the Agent in release.

  `postgresql-client` version) is open.
