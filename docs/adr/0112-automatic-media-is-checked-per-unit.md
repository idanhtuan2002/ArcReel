# Automatic media is checked per unit, repaired per unit and chosen by a ranking judge

Decided 2026-11-08 by the main agent under the sponsor's instruction to research, choose the best option and implement without asking ("tự nghiên cứu, chọn phương án tốt nhất, không cần dừng lại hỏi"). Research: [best-of-N and judge-retry donors](../research/2026-11-08-r2-best-of-n-and-judge-retry-donors.md). It extends [ADR-0111](0111-autonomous-production-run-authority.md) inside the MEDIA and QUALITY owners; it grants no new operation and changes no Human contract.

Before this decision an automatic run judged only the whole final. MEDIA treated a storyboard, clip or voice line as done once its file existed, and QUALITY findings named the episode. An automatic REWORK to MEDIA therefore regenerated nothing and ended `REWORK_WITHOUT_CHANGE` (2026-11-03 run). Donors judge at the unit: DramaClaw ranks candidates per beat and maps episode findings back to beats; OpenMontage and ViMax retry one asset a bounded number of times.

1. **Deterministic unit checks with bounded retry.** After MEDIA's storyboards exist, each storyboard image must decode, have the project orientation and at least the contract's short side, and not be blank (mean luminance inside 8..247 and standard deviation above 6). After narration audio exists, each line must decode, last at least 0.5 s, not be silent (mean volume above -50 dB) and contain no silence of 2 s or longer. A failing unit is regenerated by explicit id (a storyboard also forces its clip). Each unit has at most `MEDIA_UNIT_RETRY_LIMIT` (2) regenerations per generation; a unit still failing after that blocks the run `MEDIA_IMAGE_CHECK_FAILED` / `MEDIA_TTS_CHECK_FAILED`. Fail closed rather than "keep the best version": three deterministic failures in a row mean a misconfigured route, which a Human must see. No model judges these checks, so no qualification applies.
2. **QUALITY findings name the segment.** The reviewers' frame rules keep their episode-level thresholds (qualification semantics unchanged), but a finding is emitted once per offending segment with `unit_key = episode:<n>:segment:<id>`, the segment found from the render manifest's unit order and durations. Free-form model findings and subtitle findings keep the episode key. The M10 observation locator stays `code@whole`, so recorded evidence keeps its shape.
3. **Automatic REWORK to MEDIA regenerates the named segments.** At a generation above 1, under `quality_gate = enforce`, MEDIA reads the previous generation's stored reviews for its target; when both reviewers rejected, it regenerates the storyboard and then forces the clip of every segment a MEDIA-repairable CRITICAL or MAJOR finding names, once per generation, before ordinary planning. A Human FINAL_FILM REWORK (both reviewers accepted) and any `record` run regenerate nothing automatically: Human replacements win. `unit_repair_limit` per segment and `final_evaluation_limit` still bound the loop.
4. **Best-of-N storyboards with a ranking-only judge.** A launch profile may set `image_selection.candidates` (2 or 3) only together with the judge's model identity, prompt digest and the digest of a qualification evidence file in the repository. The run freezes `image_candidates` into its policy (the field is left out of the policy digest at its default of 1, so earlier policies keep their digests). MEDIA then makes the extra versions of each storyboard one after another (one active task per asset), asks the run's reviewer route that carries the pinned identity to score every candidate alone — the probability of "yes" to whether the picture shows its narration line — and restores the strictly highest-scoring candidate as the current version. The judge only ranks: it cannot reject output, a tie, a missing score or any judge error keeps the first candidate, and a failure never blocks the run. Its qualification is a pairwise test (the segment's own storyboard against another segment's): the own storyboard must score higher in at least 90% of at least 30 pairs, on a holdout split the method and model were not chosen on. This lighter bar is acceptable because a wrong pick costs no more than N = 1. It is never recorded as a final-review qualification and never counts as one.

Asking for a choice between two pictures in one message was tried first and rejected: on the 40-pair calibration split `qwen2.5vl:7b` answered with the same letter in both orders for 32 pairs (position, not content). Scoring one picture per question removes the order; on the same split it ranked the own storyboard higher in 35 of 40 pairs for `gemma3:4b` and `gemma3:12b` and 29 for `qwen2.5vl:7b` (`qwen3-vl:8b` returned no token probabilities). Evidence: `docs/r2/evidence/image-judge-qualification-2026-11-08/`.

**Outcome 2026-11-08: no local judge qualifies; image selection stays off.** On the disjoint 40-pair holdout split (scenes 3–4 of the same 20 stories, `holdout-pairs.json`), `gemma3:4b` scored the own storyboard higher in only 26 pairs (14 wrong, 35% against the 10% bar; `holdout-gemma3-4b.json`), so the calibration result did not generalize. No other model was tried on the holdout, which would make it a calibration split. No launch profile pins an `image_selection`; runs keep one storyboard per segment. A stronger judge (for example a cloud reviewer) is qualified by rerunning `scripts/r2/build_image_judge_corpus.py` for a fresh split and `scripts/r2/qualify_image_judge.py`, then pinning the evidence file.

Every regeneration and choice is evidence: each regeneration is a write-ahead owner attempt of the MEDIA step under a per-unit sub key (`unit:<collection>:<id>`, `unit:videos:<id>`, `rework:storyboards:<id>`, `select:<id>:v<n>`, `select:<id>:chosen`), and judge choices with every candidate's score are written to `image_selection.json` in the episode's render folder of that generation. The image judge stays disabled (`candidates = 1`) in a profile until its qualification evidence is committed.
