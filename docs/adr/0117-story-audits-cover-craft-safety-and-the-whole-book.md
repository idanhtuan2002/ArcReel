# Story audits cover craft, safety and the whole book

Decided 2026-11-09 by the main agent under the sponsor's instruction to inherit the donors' audit skills (step 3 of the long-novel plan). Research: [long-novel and skill gaps](../research/2026-11-09-r2-donor-long-novel-and-skill-gaps.md) §2.3 and §4(b)–(c). Builds on [ADR-0116](0116-long-stories-are-written-part-by-part-from-a-book-plan.md).

Before this decision a chapter was judged on four dimensions (continuity, chapter memo, character and motivation, machine voice) plus deterministic stock-phrase checks, and nothing looked across chapters, at reader pull, dialogue, point of view, harmful content, the book's hooks, a continuation's voice or a variant's faithfulness to its source. shenbi spreads these over 18 audit skills, mostly prompt-only; huohuo has a craft audit, a commercial-appeal audit and a compliance veto. Adopted here is what a verifier can hold to evidence; scoring gates and Chinese web-fiction timing rules stay out (research §4 "What NOT to adopt").

1. **Craft is one more judged dimension.** `CRAFT` reads a chapter for reader pull (the opening pulls within its first paragraph; a chapter that does not end the story ends on a concrete pull, never a pressure release), dialogue (exposition through dialogue, characters who all sound alike, lines that do nothing), point of view (the narrator knows what the point-of-view character cannot) and the chapter's turning point (rushed or summarized instead of dramatized). Each finding quotes the chapter verbatim like every other audit (shenbi `review-group-craft`, `review-reader-pull`, `review-dialogue`, `review-pov`, `review-highpoint`; huohuo `drama_gates`). A craft finding is repaired like any other inside the chapter's review cycles; one still open when the chapter is sealed is recorded as stylistic, because taste must not stop an automatic run.
2. **Safety is a judged veto.** `SAFETY` reads a chapter for sexual content involving minors, explicit sexual description, gratuitous gore or torture in detail, a method of self-harm or suicide, instructions for drugs or weapons, and hate speech; a story whose variant profile is `young-readers` also flags graphic violence and coarse language (huohuo `compliance`, shenbi `review-sensitivity`). A critical finding is a hard integrity defect: it is repaired, its claim is read a second time, and if it stays the run stops. A major one goes to the Human as an exception, which an automatic run never accepts.
3. **Cross-chapter checks run in code over the manuscript.** Each is recorded as stylistic, since its thresholds are not calibrated on Vietnamese text:
   - **Long-span repetition:** the share of a chapter's four-syllable phrases already used in earlier chapters, flagged above 12 % (shenbi `review-long-span`, six-character n-grams for Chinese).
   - **Openings:** three chapters in a row open with the same two words, or more than 40 % of a chapter's paragraphs start with the same word (shenbi `chapter-pattern`).
4. **The book's hooks are checked against what the chapters did.** A hook the plan pays in a chapter whose digest does not resolve it is recorded and stays open, so the memory keeps asking the following chapters for it (ADR-0116). In the book's last part, the final scene is rewritten once with the hooks still open; whatever stays open is recorded.
5. **A continuation keeps its voice.** The new part's sentence length, paragraph length and share of dialogue are compared in code with the text it continues; a deviation over 50 % on any of them is recorded (shenbi `sequel-writing` drift detection, without its system-term density alarm).
6. **A variant is checked against its source spine.** After the chapter plans, a judged `variant_review` lists the source events (the canon lock's `chapter_events`) the plans drop or contradict without the variant instruction asking for it, by index. Its first verdict with any is sent back to the outline as an automatic note, once; what remains is an exception for the Human (shenbi `review-fanfic`, `review-spinoff`, `canon-import` keep and change lists).

Cost: two more calls per chapter review and one per variant; the call bound grows with them.
